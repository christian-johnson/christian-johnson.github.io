Welcome to my personal site! You can find an up-to-date list of my publications and projects in the menu above. I also occasionally write blog posts.

**About me:** after receiving a PhD in physics from the University of California, Santa Cruz in 2019, I became interested in public policy, and went to work for the [RAND Corporation](https://www.rand.org/pubs/authors/j/johnson_christian.html) in Washington, DC. Over time, I became more and more interested in elections and democracy issues, which led me to my current role at [VoteShield](https://voteshield.us) (part of [Protect Democracy](https://protectdemocracy.org)).

Outside of work, I’m passionate about Ultimate frisbee, public transit, and bicycling.

**About this website:** This site is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io), using a slightly modified [Blowfish](https://blowfish.page/) template with the [Nord](https://www.nordtheme.com/docs/colors-and-palettes) color palette. It is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages).
